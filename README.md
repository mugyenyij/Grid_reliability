
### Some of the datasets we used in our analysis are publicly available while others are not. 
Below we list the datasets we used in our analysis and how to access them. 

EICV survey data:- This can be accessed from website of the National Institute of Statitics of Rwanda (https://www.statistics.gov.rw/datasource/integrated-household-living-conditions-survey-eicv)
Please note that GPS locations are not available in the public datasets. The authors had to travel to Rwanda to access this data under conditions detailed in our paper

Grid reliability data:- Please note this dataset is not publicly available. The authors through a data sharing agreement accessed this dataset from the Rwanda Energy Group

Lighting Data:- This dataset can be accessed from (https://search.earthdata.nasa.gov/search?portal=ghrc). More details are provided in the paper

Rainfall Data:- The authors had to submit a request to the Rwanda Meteo Agency to access this data. Details of the process are included in the paper
